What I work on

Reliability as a management decision

Every maintenance choice is a trade-off between cost, availability and risk. I make that trade-off explicit, measurable and defensible.

Decisions you can defend

Predictive maintenance and AI raise hundreds of alerts; people decide which ones matter. I work on making those decisions traceable, including the alerts that are set aside, with the same discipline the Oil & Gas industry applies to near misses.
